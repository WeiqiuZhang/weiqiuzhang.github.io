<p class="research-overview__eyebrow">Research focus</p>

<p class="research-overview__statement">My research focuses on global–local misalignment in education, examining how global educational concepts and standards are interpreted and implemented in local contexts. The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) provide a starting point for this line of research. My earlier work examined how sustainability agendas are adapted in higher education systems and policy; my current work turns to how they take shape in specific educational practices. Separately, drawing on my past teaching experience, I explore how game-based learning (GBL) can help students engage more deeply with literary texts. Both areas reflect my broader interest in SDG 4 and educational inequity.</p>

<p class="research-overview__scope">This page presents four completed projects at different stages of conference presentation, journal review, and submission.</p>

  <h3 id="research-cipp">Evaluating Sustainable Campuses based on CIPP Model -- a case study between Japan and China’s universities</h3>
</header>

<div class="research-project__meta">
  <p><span>Status</span><span class="research-project__meta-value"><strong>Oral Presentation</strong></span></p>
  <p><span>Venue</span><span class="research-project__meta-value"><a href="https://iprrfss.com/">2025 IPRRFSS (International Postgraduate Roundtable and Research Forum cum Summer School)</a>, EdUHK, Hong Kong SAR, 2025 Jul.</span></p>

Here are the outline of five endings and extra story:

<ul class="game-endings">
  <li><strong>不复得路(主结局)</strong>: 人类的欲望与桃花源是否可以兼得? (Main ending:Can human desires and the utopian society coexist?)</li>
  <li><strong>南柯一梦</strong>: 理想社会是否只存在于人们的想象中? (Does the utopian society only exist in imagination?)</li>
  <li><strong>雨夜梦醒</strong>: 外界的介入是否会打破桃花源的资源平衡? (Does external interference threaten the delicate harmony of resources in the utopian society?)</li>
  <li><strong>大隐隐于世</strong>: 桃花源的理想与现实生活的妥协。(A balance between the dream of seclusion and the demands of real life.)</li>
  <li><strong>不知有汉</strong>: 时空错位催生了海市蜃楼般的桃花源幻想。(The dislocation of time and space creates a mirage-like illusion of the utopian society.)</li>
  <li><strong>南阳刘子骥</strong>: 个人追求与社会舆论的矛盾。(The conflict between personal aspirations and societal opinions.)</li>
</ul>
